## Leaf surface pass, pending visual review

The live game now assigns several distinct leaf surface treatments through `game/Render/FloraSurfaceProfiles.cs` and `game/Shaders/flora.gdshader`: soft matte Catalpa-like Umbraheart leaves; mottled silvery Kiteleaf undersides; glossy pale-veined climbing ivy; wax-bloomed flecked succulents; parallel striations on needles and reeds; and fine fern venation. The treatments vary pigment pattern, simulated vein relief, and roughness rather than changing geometry. Reference cues: *Catalpa speciosa* soft pubescent undersides, *Hedera helix* glossy leaves and pale veins, and waxy succulent leaves. These are candidate materials, not user-approved finishes. Captures: `game/build/specimens/leaf-surface-final/kiteleaf/kiteleaf-mature.png`, `game/build/specimens/leaf-surface-final/umbraheart/umbraheart-mature.png`, and `game/build/specimens/leaf-surface-review/glassfinger/glassfinger-mature.png`. Both the current `dotnet build game/Vivarium.csproj` and staged Kiteleaf/Glassfinger species views passed.

### Photographed leaf material pilot, pending visual review

The shader now accepts a color, tangent-space normal, and roughness map for each species. `FloraSurfaceProfiles.Scan` binds a selected tissue region of a photographed atlas to the existing blade UVs, with per-species tint, blend, and normal strength. The meshes still determine silhouette; photographed texture supplies surface variation. Non-pilot species retain their existing procedural materials. The three pilot scan sets are CC0 Poly Haven assets credited in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`: Potted Plant 01 for Umbraheart's broad-leaf tissue, Tree Small 02 for Kiteleaf's small oval leaves, and Crystalline Iceplant for Glassfinger's succulent tissue. They are surface sources, not botanical identity claims.
