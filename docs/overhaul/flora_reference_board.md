# Flora realism: photographic reference board

Purpose: visual grounding for the 33-species flora overhaul. Each pair shows a broad growth habit and a close structural cue. These are references to study, not texture assets to copy. Preserve Vivarium's fictional species identities and ecological behavior; verify licenses separately before incorporating any source image into the game. Direct photo links are paired with their source pages for attribution and context.

## How to use this board

For each species: study its two structural references, sketch three silhouettes, build one mature hero specimen, and compare it against the current Vivarium capture in neutral light. Then validate juvenile and stressed forms, a natural cluster, and the species' habitat view. Do not project the reference photograph onto the mesh or copy a copyrighted image as a texture.
