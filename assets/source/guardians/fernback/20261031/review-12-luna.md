# Revision 12 visual anatomy review

## Verdict

Revision 12 is a substantial improvement over revision 06, and the two user-raised anatomy concerns have been addressed in the inspected renders:

1. **Overall proportions:** the skull has appropriate presence relative to the shortened trunk, the croup slopes down more clearly, and the neck cap no longer ends as a hard cutoff beneath the skull. The torso has a more readable thorax-to-loin-to-pelvis sequence than revisions 06 or 11.
2. **Limb-joint differentiation:** the limbs no longer read as undifferentiated tapered tubes. Hind stifle and hock direction are visible in the clean full-frame clay image; the forelimbs show upper-to-lower segment changes without the detached spheres that marred revision 08.

I consider the criticism materially resolved enough to move this candidate to art acceptance review. That is not a claim of photorealism: the broad, smooth guardian styling remains, and there are still small refinements to consider. The remaining concerns are not regressions to the old body proportions or smooth-pillar limbs.

## Evidence and comparison

I inspected `revision-12/face.png`, `alert_side.png`, `alert_threequarter.png`, `walking.png`, `sniffing.png`, `watchful.png`, `rear.png`, `forelimb.png`, `hindlimb.png`, `alert_side-clay.png`, `forelimb-clay-full.png`, and `hindlimb-clay-full.png`. I compared these with `revision-06/alert_side.png`, `revision-11/alert_side.png`, `scratch/fernback_realism/references/photo_hobart_1933.jpg`, and `photo_tasmanian_wolf.jpg`.

### Proportions: resolved at the structural level

The side view now has a clear, longer head wedge flowing into the neck; revision 06 had a small-looking head on a long neck and an overextended, level torso. Relative to 11, revision 12’s shorter trunk and lower croup make the back and rear silhouette more thylacine-like. `alert_side-clay.png` confirms that the throat transition is now continuous rather than ending in a visible cap seam. The ribcage remains deep, while the lower flank narrows toward the loin and the top line descends into the croup.

The body is still smoother and more uniformly full through the middle than the live side reference. The live animal has a more pronounced waist and more visibly separated pelvic plane. Revision 12 nevertheless has enough taper and posterior slope that the original “cylindrical torso” concern is no longer the dominant read. This residual difference reads as a stylization/art-direction choice rather than a structural anatomy failure.

### Limb joints: resolved at the structural level

`hindlimb-clay-full.png` shows a defined thigh, forward stifle, backward hock, and narrower distal segment. The landmarks are changes in angle and profile rather than rounded knobs. The forelimbs remain comparatively straight in the alert stance, as they should, but `forelimb-clay-full.png` shows the upper arm, elbow transition, forearm, and wrist narrowing; the elbow is subtle in a straight weight-bearing pose, not absent. The walking render confirms the joints articulate as a chain rather than a single rotating tube.

The previous lower-foreleg ball and spherical patella/hock defects are absent. Do not strengthen the bony landmarks by adding isolated bumps again. The paws are broad and somewhat rounded, with toes/claws present but not strongly separated at this full-body scale; a little more toe definition in the side silhouette could help, provided it does not make the paw oversized.

## Remaining refinements

- Consider a modest waist/pelvis plane refinement if a closer match to the photo is desired, while retaining the guardian’s deliberately clean coat and silhouette.
- At gameplay distance, improve the visible separation of the paw digits slightly; current paws can still read as rounded pads from far away.
- The eye remains glossy and bead-like and the mouth seam reads as a stylized line in `face.png`. These are facial style choices and secondary to the anatomical concerns addressed here.

The studio framing is adequate for judging the silhouette, and the clean clay views support the color-render observations. I found no remaining large, actionable anatomy defect on the scale of revision 06’s head/neck/trunk imbalance or its smooth, undifferentiated limb chains. Runtime/native integration is outside this visual-art verdict.
