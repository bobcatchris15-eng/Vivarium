# Fernback anatomical and visual references

Reviewed 2026-10-31. Original mesh authored in Blender; reference photographs and scientific figures are not embedded in the deliverable mesh or textures.

- Warburton, Travouillon and Camens (2019), [Skeletal atlas of the Thylacine](https://www.palaeo-electronica.org/content/2019/2542-thylacine-skeletal-atlas), DOI 10.26879/947. Primary anatomical study: cranium, vertebrae, shoulder and pelvic limbs. Used to guide skull length, shoulder mass, joint ordering and the substantial caudal skeleton. Behavioral interpretation from bones is uncertain; this model does not claim to reconstruct extinct-animal gait scientifically.
- [Museums Victoria articulated skeleton](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/specimens/131539), including hindlimb/tail views. Used as a comparative structural reference.
- [National Museum of Australia, Finding thylacine](https://www.nma.gov.au/explore/blog/finding-thylacine). Historical photographs and preserved anatomical collections; used to distinguish living-animal posture from mounted taxidermy.
- Existing local reference photographs inspected directly: `scratch/fernback_realism/references/photo_benjamin_1933.jpg` and `skeleton_grant_museum.jpg`. The former shows a living animal's torso depth, rounded pinnae, hindlimb angles and tapering tail. The latter supports skull, shoulder and ribcage form. Their existing filenames/provenance are retained; these are visual references, not redistributed texture sources.
- Approved project brief: `docs/2026-10-29-guardian-roster.md`. Warm grey-brown coat, pale throat/ventrum, charcoal branching hindquarter stripes, muted rose pinnae. Localized fantasy feature: six fern-like flank markings.

The model is a believable fictional guardian inspired by these references. Proportions, coat colors and poses are artistic choices, not measured reconstructions of a particular museum specimen.
