# Organ look candidate 5

Candidate 4 macro renders showed angular leaf outlines, a strong central crease and cone-like flowers. The close-detail lamina now interpolates the existing authored width profile with a monotone cubic, uses 18 longitudinal spans instead of 14, and softens the raised midrib to a broad shallow crown. Existing cross curl, edge wave, twist, colors and clockwise winding remain in use. Flowers use a late mouth flare and shallow five-lobed rim, with 10 radial sides and seven length spans at close detail; fruit geometry and attachment radius remain unchanged. Existing outward-normal calculation remains in place.

Per close-detail shape, leaf geometry changes from 75 to 95 vertices and 112 to 144 triangles; flower geometry changes from 49 to 81 vertices and 88 to 150 triangles. These meshes are shared per shape; instance counts do not change. Medium/far tessellation is unchanged, although their width interpolation and flower profile also change. Root subsequently built and inspected matched native macros in `build/plant-graph-tuning-20261115/candidate5`, retaining the smoother outline and mouth. This was a bounded shape improvement, not final photoreal material acceptance. The full catalog continuation is documented in `experiments/plant_catalog_test/README.md`.
