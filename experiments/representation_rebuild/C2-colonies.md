# C2 colony recovery: partial visual acceptance

The current experiment shades four colony coverage channels (moss, crustose, foliose and fruticose) on the displayed substrate top mesh, with separate health, relative thickness and moisture channels. Binding is opt-in; unbound substrate renderers keep the existing material. The existing Moss002 and LichenThallus assets supply material detail; the lichen scan is triplanar on rock. Near detail adds deterministic attached microstructure, while far detail uses the field without separate colony geometry. This is an experimental demo, not production island integration. The demo holds substrate water fixed during growth and separately forces a dry state; it does not establish coupled evolving hydrology.

The latest 15-shot native run completed under `build/c2/scanned/` with `REPR_REVIEW_COMPLETE`; commands and per-shot data are in `build/c2/scanned.log` and `report.json`. Root and worker directly reviewed the mature whole, rock and wet-macro views. The scanned material adds recognizable lichen lobes compared with the earlier smooth coat, and moss reads as a low textured mat with small leafy shoots rather than grass. Visual acceptance remains **partial**: the rock still carries an overly broad pale coating, with a pale basal band and small-rock halo. Early/mature/dry and LOD images are retained for comparison; no quantitative rendered LOD equivalence or visual-quality acceptance is claimed.

The `local_edit` assertion succeeded: branch-lichen area increased by 0.004260 m², one dirty record was published, one of four substrate field textures uploaded, and one microstructure tile invalidated and rebuilt (`build/c2/scanned.log`). Narrowing the signature to the self/west/south/southwest tiles actually read by the corner sampler reduced this fixture's prior four micro invalidations to one. This one-metre, four-tile case does not by itself prove whole-island locality.

The previous complete colony suite passed 20/20 (`build/c2/leafy-final-tests.log`), including edge-thickness interpolation and unchanged-coverage appearance checks. Mature interior coverage was 0.536 versus rim 0.231. Following the lichen geometry revision, focused micro checks passed 3/3 (`build/c2/lichen-micro.log`): deterministic geometry and attributes, matching-detail sampled rendered-vertex contact, and a warm all-level fixture build of 72.9 ms for 43,164 triangles against the existing 80 ms gate. Earlier runs measured 34.0 and 75.6 ms; these variable Debug fixture timings do not establish general performance. The subsequent full representation run included all colony checks and passed them; its sole failure was the unrelated substrate timing gate (73 total, one failure). The worker receipt is `build/reviews/resume-20261113/coverage-full-suite.log`; the full raw console transcript was not separately saved. Sampled vertices do not prove triangle-interior clearance or exact microstructure contact when substrate and colony independently select different LODs.

The latest joint solution and game builds passed (`build/c2/scanned-solution.log`, `build/c2/scanned-game.log`); the solution reported nullable warnings in LiveDiagnostics and PlantFieldDemo, and the game build reported none. The 1600×900 native review ran with the default 30 fps cap, so its CPU frame times are pacing-limited and its GPU timings are diagnostic rather than an uncapped comparison.

Renderer-created substrate meshes, materials, images and textures now have explicit exit/reinitialization cleanup; in-flight managed builders are waited out and queued results discarded without committing on exit. Shared `GD.Load` assets are not disposed. The native run still reported 7 Texture RID and 1 ObjectDB warning. A stock-lighting harness control without the colony or specimen substrate renderer (`build/c2/harness-baseline.log`) reported the same counts; those counts do not identify individual RIDs or establish safety when exiting with pending builders. A dedicated pending-work/reinitialization probe remains unrun.

The former standalone colony sheets failed sampled contact against actual 1 cm substrate triangles (149/12,459 base samples more than 2 mm below, worst 8.8 mm), produced visible pale sheets and had independent-LOD contact problems, so they were rejected. The sheet source, obsolete sheet-specific checks and failure evidence are preserved under `experiments/representation_rebuild/rejected/C2-sheets/`. Those checks were replaced by field-state, microstructure determinism/LOD/cost and sampled rendered-vertex checks; the retired engine-neutral optical proxy did not model the final shader or lighting and is not evidence of rendered LOD equivalence.
